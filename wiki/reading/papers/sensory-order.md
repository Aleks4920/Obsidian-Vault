# The Sensory Order

**Author:** F.A. Hayek  
**Topic:** Hayek's theory of mind — sensory qualities as relational classifications  
**Related:** [[intelligence-edge-of-chaos]]

---

Not yet read (Status: Not started in Notion). No notes recorded.

**Context:** 1952 book by economist and philosopher F.A. Hayek proposing a theory of mind and perception. Argues that sensory qualities are not intrinsic properties of stimuli but emerge from the **relational structure** of the nervous system's classification processes — anticipating connectionist theories of mind. Relevant to understanding emergence of intelligence from structure.
