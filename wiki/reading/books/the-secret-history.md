**Context:** Donna Tartt's debut novel (1992). A dark academia thriller following a group of classics students at a small Vermont college who, under the influence of their charismatic professor, commit a murder — and must live with the consequences. Explores themes of moral corruption, intellectual elitism, guilt, and the seductiveness of beauty and aestheticism.
