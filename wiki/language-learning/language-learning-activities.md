# Language Learning Activities

Language learning activities and practice methods. Part of the [[language-learning]] system.

## Activity Types

- **Speaking practice** — conversation sessions, shadowing, output drills
- **Listening exercises** — podcasts, audio lessons, immersion content
- **Reading in target language** — graded readers, native-level texts
- **Writing exercises** — journaling, sentence construction, translation
- **Vocabulary drills** — flashcards, spaced repetition (SRS)
- **Grammar practice** — targeted exercises, pattern drilling

Detailed activity log is tracked in the Notion Activities database at the [Notion source](https://www.notion.so/98d78d9d577348eeab5fc53d5fa9ece4).
