# The Creative Act: A Way of Being

**Author:** Rick Rubin  
**Format:** Physical book  
**Related:** [[books]], [[benjamin-franklin-autobiography]]

---

## Notes

- Everything that is created is art
- Ideas want to manifest and will go to someone else if you don't act on them
- Try to be aware of your experiences and look deeply at things
- Attempt to take in raw data — don't filter what you feel will be important; remember things and their beauty
- Pay attention to your environment and clues that may be dropped spiritually
- Don't copy great works — instead see **why** they are great

## Themes

- Creativity as a receptive state, not just active production
- Ideas as external forces seeking expression through a willing creator
- Deep attention and observation as the foundation of creative work
- Understanding greatness through analysis rather than imitation
