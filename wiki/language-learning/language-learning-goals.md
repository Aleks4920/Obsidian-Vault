# Language Learning Goals

Language learning goals tracker. This page links to the [[language-learning]] hub and tracks specific targets for language acquisition.

## Goal Categories

- **Language proficiency milestones** — e.g. A1 → B2 progression targets
- **Vocabulary targets** — word count goals per language
- **Grammar mastery** — specific structures to acquire
- **Speaking/listening fluency goals** — conversational and comprehension benchmarks

Detailed goals are tracked in the Notion Language Goals database at the [Notion source](https://www.notion.so/ec2aa01d3d674e59aacf54eb085dda2f).
