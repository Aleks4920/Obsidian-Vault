# Tender Is the Flesh

**Author:** Agustina Bazterrica  
**Genre:** Dystopian Fiction  
**Related:** [[books]], [[the-secret-history]]

---

> "There are words that are convenient, hygienic. Legal"

## Notes

- Easy to dehumanize when government tells you to and you are desperate
- Slavery is illegal but raising humans for cannibalism is not (the "legal" loophole)
- Vegans are frowned upon in this society
- Eating "meat" (humans) is expected and normalized by society
- There are riots against animals and art depicting animals
- The virus and cannibalism become part of religion

## Themes

- Language as a tool of dehumanization — euphemisms make the unthinkable palatable
- How society normalizes atrocity when it is institutionalized and profitable
- The fragility of moral frameworks under systemic pressure
- Critique of the meat industry through extreme analogy
