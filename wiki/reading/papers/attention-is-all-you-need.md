# Attention Is All You Need

**Authors:** Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, Aidan N. Gomez, Łukasz Kaiser, Illia Polosukhin  
**Topic:** The Transformer architecture — self-attention based sequence model

**Context:** Landmark 2017 paper introducing the **Transformer** architecture — entirely based on self-attention mechanisms, replacing recurrent and convolutional layers. Foundation for all modern large language models (GPT, BERT, T5, etc.).

Key concepts: multi-head self-attention, positional encoding, encoder-decoder architecture, scaled dot-product attention.
