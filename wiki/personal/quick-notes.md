# Quick Notes

Miscellaneous quick notes and interesting tidbits collected over time.

## Notes

- **Hippocampus & Dopamine**: The hippocampus remembers things based on dopamine. To remember stuff better, get dopamine from small things. Switch up your daily life to create novel dopamine-triggering situations.

- **80/20 Rule for Non-Fiction Books**: Non-fiction books have most of the core takeaways or knowledge in 20% of the book. The rest is examples and filler.

- **Shareholder Primacy**: Companies have a moral obligation to maximize shareholder value. Started with Ford after he tried to make better work conditions — was overruled by the principle that doing what's best for the company (even if stock goes down) overrides workers' interests.

- **Project Gunman (1984)**: A project to replace all devices from the US Embassy in Russia to stop persistent bugging. Everything was tamper-proofed. Took 100+ days.
