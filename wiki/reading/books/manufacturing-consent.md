# Manufacturing Consent

**Author:** Noam Chomsky (and Edward S. Herman)  
**Status:** In progress  
**Related:** [[books]], [[propaganda]]

---

Currently reading. No notes yet.

**Context:** Landmark work of political economy analyzing how mass media in democratic societies functions to manufacture public consent for the interests of powerful elites. Introduces the **Propaganda Model** — five "filters" that shape what gets reported: ownership, advertising revenue, sourcing, flak, and anti-communism (later updated to fear of "enemy"). Companion to [[propaganda]] by Edward Bernays.
