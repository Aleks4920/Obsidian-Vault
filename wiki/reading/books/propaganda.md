# Propaganda

**Author:** Edward Bernays  
**Format:** Audiobook  
**Related:** [[books]], [[manufacturing-consent]]

---

No notes recorded in Notion.

**Context:** 1928 book by Edward Bernays — Freud's nephew and the "father of public relations." Argues that the conscious manipulation of the organized habits and opinions of the masses is an important element in democratic society. Explains how propaganda techniques (drawn from psychology and advertising) shape public opinion. Bernays saw propaganda as a tool for social management, not just wartime use. Closely related to Chomsky's [[manufacturing-consent]].
