# HECKLER: Breaking Confidential VMs with Malicious Interrupts

**Authors:** Benedict Schlüter, Supraja Sridhara, Mark Kuhne, Andrin Bertschi, Shweta Shinde  
**Topic:** Attack breaking confidential VMs (AMD SEV, Intel TDX) using malicious interrupt injection  
**Related:** [[cybersecurity-notes]], [[reflections-trusting-trust]]

---

No notes recorded in Notion.

**Context:** HECKLER is a 2024 security paper demonstrating attacks on **Confidential Virtual Machines** (CVMs) — a hardware-based isolation technology (AMD SEV-SNP, Intel TDX). The attack exploits the hypervisor's ability to inject interrupts into a CVM to break confidentiality guarantees.
