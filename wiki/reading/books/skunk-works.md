**Author:** Ben Rich and Leo Janos  
**Format:** Audiobook

---

No notes recorded in Notion.

**Context:** Account of Lockheed's Skunk Works division — the secretive advanced development team responsible for the U-2, SR-71 Blackbird, and F-117 Stealth Fighter. Written by Ben Rich, who succeeded Kelly Johnson as director. Covers the culture of radical engineering innovation, secrecy, and rapid prototyping that defined one of the most productive engineering teams in history.
